What we found in the brief

The account exists and both channels are on the table. The risk is that $500 split evenly across Search and LSA gives neither enough data to judge. Three things decide whether this test produces an answer.

$500 needs staging, not splitting

High impact

LSA verification and the Google Guaranteed checks take time, while Search can run from day one. Running them in sequence lets each channel collect enough leads to compare.

Leads must be attributed to the right channel

High impact

LSA leads, Search calls, website forms and Business Profile calls can all look like "a call came in". If they are not separated, nobody can say which channel deserves the next dollar.

Common setup
  • One generic "call" conversion
  • Page view counted as a lead
  • LSA leads reviewed by hand
  • No record of which leads booked
What this plan sets up
  • Separate call, form and LSA lead actions
  • Minimum call length on call conversions
  • Business Profile linked and tagged
  • Booked inspection status fed back weekly

Intent differs by service

Medium impact

Repair and storm searches tend to be urgent and close to a booking. Replacement searches are higher value but slower and more competitive, so on a small test budget they come in once cost per lead is known.

1

Audit the existing account

Campaigns, targeting, keywords, search terms, negatives, location settings and every conversion action currently counted.

Why: no budget moves until we know what the account is optimizing toward today.
2

Rebuild tracking by channel

Separate actions for Search calls, website forms and LSA leads. Calls counted only above a minimum length. Business Profile connected.

Why: the test only works if each booked inspection traces back to one channel.
3

Set up or tighten LSA

Eligibility, business verification, license and insurance checks, service categories, service area, weekly budget and a lead handling routine for fast answers and disputes.

Why: LSA charges per lead, so answer rate and disputing invalid leads protect the budget.
4

Launch focused Search

Tight radius, repair and storm inspection ad groups, call and form extensions, negatives for DIY, jobs and materials searches.

Why: urgent intent produces leads fastest, which gives Search enough data in two weeks.
5

Compare, report, decide

Weekly report on spend, leads, booked inspections and lead quality. Day 30 recommendation on which channel gets the next budget and by how much.

Why: you approve any significant change before it happens.
